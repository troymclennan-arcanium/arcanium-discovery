The story

You asked for a combination of Potion and Die-Cut. We did not just stick them together. We kept the round, approachable flask you liked in Potion, and the white sticker edge and solid weight you loved in Die-Cut, then let the bubbles escape over the edge so you can see them.

What we took from you
  • Your words: "a combination of the potion and die-cut themes".
  • Your words: "I loved the idea of the bubble", so the bubbles now leave the flask.
  • Your words: "the rounded flask feels more approachable".
How we shaped it
  • A round flask body with a white sticker edge all round.
  • Bubbles that break over the edge, so they are seen.
  • A wider colour range: pink, coral, lemon, lime, aqua and violet.

Who it is designed for

Square spaces: social profile pictures, signs on a van or a stall, stickers.

Horizontal, full colour

Wide spaces: website header, email signature, social cover, quotes and invoices.

Reversed, on dark

Dark grounds: a night-time story, a dark gift box, the inside of a bag.

One colour

One-colour printing, stamps, embroidery, and anywhere gradients cannot be reproduced.

Icon only

Tiny spaces: app icon, favicon, sticker, the corner of a card.

Wordmark only

When the symbol already appears nearby: a footer, a receipt, a line of text.

Using the logo well
  • Keep the sticker edge. It is part of the mark.
  • Let the bubbles break the edge. Do not trim them.
  • Use the reversed version on dark grounds.
  • Never stretch or tilt the flask.

  2. Round 23 Nov 2026You are here
    Round 2: built from what you told us

    You asked for a combination of Potion and Die-Cut and a more playful colour range. Round 2 gives you three ideas: a new one that joins them, your favourite refined, and Potion refined. Round 1 is still open.

    “I would like to see a combination of the potion and die-cut themes.”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Concept A, Fizzy Sticker, joins them. It takes Potion's round, approachable flask and Die-Cut's white sticker edge and weight, then lets the bubbles break out over the edge.

    “I'd also like to see a more playful colour range used”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Every idea now has a wider range of its own: eight colours in A, seven in B and seven in C. Round 1 covered as little as one hue in Badge. We kept the colours you loved and added new ones.

    “I loved the idea of the bubble, and the one word colour ideas, but I didnt see much of the bubble features.”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Each idea now has its own signature detail you can play with. In A the bubbles lift off and pop. In B a sticker peels. In C little bottles float up. The one word colour idea is kept, and whether the name is one colour or two is a question for you.

    “the 'reversed' colours felt more fun and bright over all.”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: The reversed version now has its own tuned set of bright colours on the dark ground in every idea, and it leads the Logo versions section.

    “i love the logo and the sticker idea.”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Kept. Concept B stays close to Die-Cut, and the sticker edge carries into A.

    “the rounded flask feels more approachable.”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Kept and used. The round flask is the body of A and the shape of C.

    “I particularly like the playful display through the instagram stlye”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Concept C puts Try it on, with the Instagram look, straight after the opening.

    “not a fan of the limited colour range and the flask shape”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Doodle is not in Round 2, and stays open in Round 1. We did not rework it. Its wider colour thinking went into the new ideas.

    “dont like the monotone colour range, but do like the badge style of icon”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Badge is not in Round 2, and stays open in Round 1. Its monotone colour is not used. We did not rebuild the badge icon, so that style is not in this round.

    • Each idea now has its own opening, its own look and its own reading order, so they feel like three different ideas and not one page in three colours.
    • The three open questions are still yours to answer: should the name be one colour or two, is it LogoLab or Logo Lab, and one typeface or several.
    • Round 1 stays open from the Rounds tab, exactly as you left it.
  3. Round 313 Nov 2026
    Round 3: one idea, the whole brand

    You loved Fizzy Sticker, so this round is only that idea, taken further. It is now the whole proposed look and feel for LogoLab: one sticker round the whole logo, the word Lab in any of six colours, and a rebuilt one colour version. Rounds 1 and 2 are still open.

    “can you refine this design”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Round 3 is Fizzy Sticker only, taken further. Die-Cut Refined and Potion Refined are not in this round, and stay open in Round 2.

    “use 'bubblegum' for the 'Lab'”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Done. The Lab word is now the real Bubblegum on the main bright version. On a white page that pink is too faint to read, so the light version keeps a deeper pink. The bright version sits on its own dark sticker, where the Bubblegum reads clearly.

    “allow the Logo 'Lab' word interchangable with the theme's colour range”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Done. Lab comes in six colours: Bubblegum, Coral, Lemon, Lime, Aqua and Electric Grape, each on a dark sticker where it reads. We tuned the violet a little brighter for the Lab word so it stays easy to read, and kept the palette violet for the flask.

    “bring the sticker edge all the way around the logo”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Done. The whole logo is now one die-cut sticker. The flask, the bubbles and the letters all sit inside one even edge.

    “The 'one colour' idea in this theme is poor”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Rebuilt. The old version turned the bubbles into black dots on a black body. The new one keeps the glass outline, the liquid and each bubble as separate white shapes.

    “the 'potion refined' one colour design is a great design”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: We took what makes it work, which is parts that stay separate, and did not copy its shape into the Fizzy flask.

    “I like the stcker idea.”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Kept. It is now the whole idea.

    • The page is one idea now, so it reads as your brand and not as a choice between ideas.
    • The three open questions are still yours to answer: should the name be one colour or two, is it LogoLab or Logo Lab, and one typeface or several.
    • Rounds 1 and 2 stay open from the Rounds tab, exactly as you left them.
    Revisit Round 3
  4. Round 413 Nov 2026
    Round 4: the Lab look, and every logo shown

    The same Fizzy Sticker idea, now on the same deep grape ground, bubbles and bright accents as your private login screen, with every logo layout shown in every colour. Rounds 1, 2 and 3 are still open from the Rounds tab.

    “now use lilac to present the page. I loved how the logo poped off it.”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Done. The logo cards sit on lilac, where the white sticker edge lifts off the ground. The rest of the page now takes its look from your private login screen: the deep grape ground, bubbles and Bubblegum accents. The Peach Cream swatch in the palette is unchanged.

    “I also need you to update the logos used and presented throughout the microsite.”
    Troy McLennan, Director

    What we did: Done. Every logo on the page is the latest set, and the page now shows all of them: the stacked, horizontal, icon and wordmark layouts, each with Lab in all six colours. Round 3 showed only the stacked ones in colour. The horizontal logo is now one sticker with a narrow slit between the flask and the word.

    • The page now takes its look from your private login screen: the deep grape ground, drifting bubbles, rounded headings and the Bubblegum buttons.
    • Key words now pop in the brand colours (Bubblegum, Coral, Lemon, Lime and Aqua), only on the dark grape, so they stay easy to read.
    • The logo cards keep the lilac ground you liked, so the white sticker edge still lifts off the page.
    • The logo versions section grew from 12 cards to 30, so every layout and colour can be seen.
    • Rounds 1, 2 and 3 are untouched and stay open from the Rounds tab.
